Biography

Saifullah Memon is a PhD researcher in Electrical Engineering and Informatics at the Budapest University of Technology and Economics (BME), Hungary, where he holds a Stipendium Hungaricum scholarship. His research focuses on neural speech decoding: using deep learning to turn intracranial EEG (iEEG) and EEG signals into speech. His work explores deep learning models for speech decoding, self-supervised representation learning for label-efficient decoding, and the reliable evaluation of imagined speech decoding systems, with the long-term goal of building practical brain–computer interfaces that can restore communication.

Before his PhD, he earned an MSc in Mechatronics Engineering from Óbuda University, Budapest, and a BE in Electronics Engineering from Mehran University of Engineering and Technology, Pakistan. He then spent three years as a Lecturer in Computer Systems Engineering at Balochistan University of Engineering and Technology (BUET), Pakistan.

At BME, he supports teaching in Deep Learning, Infocommunication and Speech Information Systems, and supervises master's students in the project lab. His first paper was published at SPECOM 2026 (Springer LNCS), and three journal articles are under review.
